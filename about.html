About the Foundation • ಆರೂಢ ಫೌಂಡೇಶನ್

A refuge for the honest question.

ಸತ್ಯಾನ್ವೇಷಿಗಳ ಆಶ್ರಯ

Aroodha Foundation exists for one purpose: to inspire and guide seekers of truth on their spiritual journeys.

Our Purpose

Taught the way it was received

We provide authentic knowledge, wisdom, and support so that individuals can discover their true potential and live a life of purpose and fulfilment. Nothing is withheld, nothing is dressed up. Practice is taught the way it was received — carefully, in sequence, with a teacher present.

From Hubballi, the Foundation holds weekly satsangs, daily yoga and pranayama classes, seasonal sadhana intensives, and mantra diksha for those who are ready. Online membership carries the same teaching to seekers wherever they are.

What We Hold To

Four commitments

01

Authenticity above novelty

We teach what was received, in the order it was received. Where a text is silent, we say so.

02

No fee for the teaching

Satsang and diksha are given by dana. Programme fees cover hall, staff, and streaming — nothing more.

03

A teacher in the room

Practice is corrected by a person, not by a video. Every stream has a named teacher and a fixed size.

04

Household first

You are not asked to renounce anything. Sadhana that cannot survive your family and job is not yet sadhana.

The Seeker's Path • ಸಾಧಕನ ಮಾರ್ಗ

How a seeker moves through

Step one ಪ್ರಶ್ನೆ

Arrive with your question

Come to a Sunday satsang. Ask whatever is actually on your mind. Nothing is expected of you.

Step two ಅಭ್ಯಾಸ

Build the foundation

Eight to twelve weeks of asana and pranayama until the body can sit still without argument.

Step three ಕ್ರಿಯಾ

Enter kriya

On the teacher’s assessment, begin Shree Kriya with monthly personal correction.

Step four ದೀಕ್ಷಾ

Receive diksha

When the ground holds, request initiation. Forty days of preparation, then the mantra — once, in person.
