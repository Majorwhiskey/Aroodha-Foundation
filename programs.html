Before You Begin

Questions seekers ask

Do I need any experience to start? add

None. Aroodha Yoga runs for seven days, free, and assumes nothing — no flexibility, no reading, no prior practice. Come as you are and follow along.

What should I bring to a morning class? add

A mat, loose clothes, and an empty stomach. Nothing else. Water is kept at the hall.

Are the sessions in Kannada or English? add

Taught in Kannada, with English explanation wherever it helps. Mantras are given in Sanskrit with their meaning explained.

Is there a fee for satsang or diksha? add

No. Satsang and mantra diksha are given by dana — offer what you wish, or nothing. Programme fees cover only hall, staff, and streaming.

Can I practise if I do not live in Hubballi? add

Yes. Online membership carries the live 5:30 AM and 7:00 PM IST sessions, the recorded library, and monthly one-to-one correction wherever you are.

How do I request mantra diksha? add

Write to us through the contact page. A forty-day preparation precedes initiation, and the date is set by the panchanga — usually a few weeks after your request.
